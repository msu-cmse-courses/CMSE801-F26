# Generative AI Use Policy Statement

The following policy is adapted from text initially generated from<sup>[1](#footnote1)

For this course, the use of generative artificial intelligence (AI) tools (such as ChatGPT, Copilot, DALL-E, etc.) is both welcomed and encouraged. Generative AI represents a fascinating intersection of computing and creativity, offering students an opportunity to explore cutting-edge technologies while honing their programming skills.  That said, do not rely solely on AI tools to complete assignments, as this will likely not support the course learning objectives, which include the critical appraisal of algorithms and the ability to break scientific problems into solvable components.  AI tools should be used only as an assistant to, not a replacement for, your own work. **Unproductive use of AI tools can significantly hinder your learning experience and diminish your understanding of fundamental concepts.**

Likewise, it is imperative that the utilization of generative AI within this course adheres to ethical guidelines and respects the intellectual property rights of others. As you engage with generative AI technologies, please keep the following principles in mind:

1. **Academic Integrity:** When using generative AI tools, it is essential to attribute the contributions of the AI appropriately. If a portion of your work is generated by AI, be transparent about its influence and contribution to your project.
In this class, you will be allowed unrestricted use of a custom generative AI course assistant, HiTA. However, when using any non-HiTA generative AI tool, you will be required to follow the interaction and attribution level **GAUC-4C**, as outlined in the [MSU Generative AI Use Codes](https://iteach.commons.msu.edu/2023/08/28/generative-ai-use-codes/):

    * *GAUC-4: Collaborative Creation with Generative AI*
    *Description:* Students can collaborate with generative AI to create content. While students should be actively involved in the creation process, they can interweave their own content with content generated by the AI.
   * *C: Comprehensive Attribution Required*
    *Description:* Students should provide a comprehensive citation, detailing not just the AI platform/tool, but also specifying parameters, prompts, or any other specifics of how the AI was utilized.
     
Properly citing both the tools and the specific prompts for each piece of generated content helps ensure transparency, reproducibility, and gives credit to the creators of the AI tools.  As an example, see the citation at the end of this page<sup>[1](#footnote1).

3. **Ethical Use:** Ensure that the generated content adheres to ethical standards and does not promote harm, discrimination, or any form of malicious intent. 

4. **Intellectual Property:** While generative AI tools can help you create novel content, they should not be used to plagiarize or infringe upon the intellectual property rights of others. Make sure that the content generated is original and properly cited if it draws from existing sources.

5. **Algorithm Understanding:** While you may be using pre-built generative AI libraries and tools, strive to understand the underlying algorithms and mechanisms. This knowledge will empower you to use these tools more effectively and creatively.

6. **Experimentation and Innovation:** Generative AI offers a unique platform for experimentation and innovation. Feel free to explore unconventional ideas, pushing the boundaries of what's possible while maintaining ethical and responsible use.

7. **Collaboration:** Collaborative projects involving generative AI are encouraged, but all contributors should be credited for their individual contributions. Transparent communication about the extent of AI involvement is vital to maintain the integrity of collaborative work.

8. **Data and Privacy:** When using data with generative models, ensure that you have the necessary rights to use the data and are allowed to input data into the generative model.  All data use must follow <a href="https://tech.msu.edu/about/guidelines-policies/generative-ai/">MSU guidelines on Data Uses and Risks of Generative AI</a>. You should not use confidential, private, or any type of custom research data with current generative models.

9. **Learning Priority:** While generative AI is a valuable supplemental tool, remember that the primary focus of the course is on computational modeling and programming. The use of generative AI should supplement your learning journey rather than overshadow core concepts. 

Course policy on generative AI use will be reviewed as the technology, research, institutional guidance, and evidence about its educational impact continues to evolve.
