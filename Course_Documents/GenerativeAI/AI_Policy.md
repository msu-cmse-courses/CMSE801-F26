# AI Policy Statement
The following policy is adapted from text initially generated from<sup>[1](#footnote1)

You are encouraged to use generative AI tools in this course as collaborators in your work, including for drafting, coding, analysis, and revision, as long as you remain the author and can stand behind everything you submit. With that freedom comes responsibility. You must verify AI output, since claims, sources, data, and code are often confidently wrong. You must be able to explain and defend your work without the tool. And you must disclose how AI was used on each assignment. Submitting AI output you cannot explain, or presenting it as unaided work, is not permitted. Protect private and restricted information when using these tools, and I may ask you to share the AI conversation behind your work. The aim is for you to learn to do excellent work *with* these tools, which is itself a skill this course is helping you build.
